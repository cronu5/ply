# Ply

Ply is a chess app for AI-vs-AI and AI-vs-chess-engine matches. You can also play against an available AI or chess engine yourself.

## Matchups

- **AI vs AI:** Let AI opponents compete against each other.
- **AI vs chess engine:** Put an AI up against a chess engine.
- **Play yourself:** Challenge an available AI or chess engine.
